How the Partnership Works

Whether you work with us as a white-label partner or refer a client to SiteWithSoul, we keep the process simple from the first conversation to the final launch

01 — Choose Your Partnership Model

Start by choosing the way you'd like to work with us. With White-Label Development, you bring the project and we handle the website development behind the scenes. With a Referral Partnership, you introduce a client to us and we take care of the project directly

02 — Share the Project Brief

We discuss the project before any work begins — the client's business, goals, website requirements, pages, features, content, deadlines and budget. We can work from your existing brief, designs or requirements, and we'll clarify anything that is missing before development starts

03 — Talk to the Right Person

For White-Label projects, you remain the main contact with your client, while you provide us with the information we need to build the website.

For Referral projects, the client works directly with SiteWithSoul. This means we can ask questions, collect feedback and clarify details without passing information through a third person.

04 — Agree on the Scope & Payment

Before development starts, we agree on the project scope, timeline, deliverables and payment schedule.

For White-Label projects, the partner pays SiteWithSoul according to the agreed project schedule.

For Referral projects, the client pays SiteWithSoul directly. The partner's referral commission is paid after the corresponding client payment has been received.

05 — We Build the Website

Once everything is agreed and the required information is ready, development begins. We keep you updated on the project progress and let you know when we need feedback, approvals or additional information.

06 — Review, Launch & Continue

The website goes through a final review before launch. We take care of the agreed technical setup and make sure everything is ready to go live.

After launch, we can continue working together on maintenance, updates or future website projects.
