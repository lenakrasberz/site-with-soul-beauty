Public Offer Contract

Valid pursuant to the relevant articles of the Civil Code of Ukraine on public offer.

1. General Terms

1.1. This document constitutes an official proposal (Public Offer) by SiteWithSoul Studio (hereinafter — “Contractor”) to conclude an agreement for the provision of website development services with any individual or legal entity (hereinafter — “Customer”).

1.2. Acceptance of this Offer is deemed to be the Customer’s submission of an application on the website, via messenger, by email, or by making a prepayment.

1.3. As of the moment of acceptance, this Offer shall be considered a concluded agreement on the terms set forth below.

2. Scope of the Contract

2.1. The Contractor undertakes to perform work on the development, configuration, and deployment of a website in accordance with the Customer’s wishes, and the Customer undertakes to accept and pay for the results of the completed work.

2.2. The specific characteristics of the project (design, structure, functionality, deadlines, and cost) are determined individually — through correspondence, a brief, or a technical specification.

3. Cost and Payment Procedure

3.1. The cost of services is agreed upon individually depending on the scope of work.

3.2. Payment is made in two stages: a 50% prepayment — after the approval of project details and the start of work. The remaining 50% — after project completion and before the transfer of all materials to the Customer.

3.3. Payment is made to the Contractor’s bank card (of a Ukrainian bank) or by other agreed-upon means.

3.4. The prepayment confirms the reservation of the Contractor’s time and the commencement of the project execution. In the event the Customer cancels the project, the prepayment is non-refundable, as it serves as compensation for the time spent and project preparation.

4. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

The Contractor undertakes to:

  • Perform the work with high quality and within the agreed-upon deadlines.
  • Inform the Customer about the progress of the project execution.
  • Transfer the finished website and all access credentials after full payment.

The Contractor has the right to:

  • Postpone the start of work until the prepayment is received.
  • Feature the completed work in their portfolio.
  • Refuse the project if the Customer fails to meet the deadlines for feedback or payment.

The Customer undertakes to:

  • Provide materials and feedback in a timely manner.
  • Pay for the services according to the agreed-upon terms.
  • Not transfer access credentials to third parties without the Contractor’s consent.

5. Acceptance and Handover Procedure

5.1. Upon project completion, the Contractor sends the Customer a link to the finished website for review.

5.2. If the Customer fails to provide substantiated comments within 3 business days, the project shall be considered accepted.

5.3. All passwords, logins, and access credentials shall be transferred to the Customer after full payment.

6. Liability of the Parties

6.1. The Contractor shall not be liable for the operation of third-party services (hosting, domain, payment systems, etc.).

6.2. The Contractor shall not be liable for the consequences of the Customer’s independent changes to the website code or settings after the project handover.

6.3. The Customer shall be liable for the materials provided by them (texts, images, videos) and their compliance with the legislation.

7. Confidentiality

7.1. All data obtained during the cooperation process shall be confidential and shall not be disclosed to third parties without the Customer’s consent.

7.2. The Contractor may use the project name and website screenshots solely for portfolio demonstration purposes.

8. Term of the Offer

8.1. This Offer comes into effect from the moment of its publication on the Contractor’s website and remains valid indefinitely.

8.2. The Contractor has the right to amend the terms of the Offer at any time. The new version becomes effective from the moment of its publication.
